<objective>
Fix two table-cell rendering bugs in the native PDF reassembler:

Bug 1 — Word adhesion: adjacent cells share a border-centerline coordinate; without
an inset on the insert rect, translated text from cell0 and cell1 renders with zero
gap, causing PDF word-extraction to join them as a single token. Fix: apply a 1.5 pt
inset to the insert rect only (redact rect stays at full cell dimensions).

Bug 2 — Blank cells: insert_htmlbox with scale_low=0.7 returns spare_height=-1 and
renders NOTHING when JP→VN-expanded text in 80-120 pt wide, 12-20 pt tall cells needs
scale < 0.7 to fit. Fix: lower scale_low to 0.3 for table_cell segments only.

Purpose: Demo doc job 7f958166 (1409 segments, 98% table_cell) is the triggering
artefact — tables overflow + cells are blank on that job. After this plan the
re-run must show legible table content with no scale_low overflow flags.

Output:
- Two new pytest tests (RED → GREEN lifecycle documented in tasks below)
- Two surgical constant additions + one conditional branch in Pass 3 of reassemble_pdf
</objective>
